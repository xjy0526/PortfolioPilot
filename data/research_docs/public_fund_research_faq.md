# PortfolioPilot Fund Research FAQ

> This is a simulated public sample. It contains no employer or internship information.

## What is the purpose of the research workspace?

The workspace organizes public market data, simulated research notes, risk indicators, and review tasks. Outputs are research material and are not trade instructions.

## How should stale evidence be handled?

Evidence outside its effective period should be deactivated or assigned an effective-to date. Expired evidence must not appear in normal retrieval.

## Who can access restricted documents?

Restricted documents require an explicit permission-group match. Authorization is applied before document chunks are embedded or scored.
